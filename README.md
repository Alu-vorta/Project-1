A personal portfolio website built from scratch with **HTML, CSS and JavaScript**. It has no frameworks and no build step.

## 👋 About

Hi, I'm **Arman**, a B.Sc (Hons) Computer Science student at **Hansraj College, University of Delhi** and an **ICCR scholar**.
This site is my corner of the internet: who I am, what I'm building and how to reach me.

## 📄 Pages

| Page | What's on it |
|------|--------------|
| **Home** (`index.html`) | Animated name headline, a typewriter terminal that greets you in 5 languages, and quick links |
| **About** (`about.html`) | Education, recognition, my toolkit and what I'm currently building |
| **Contact** (`contact.html`) | Email and social links, plus a contact form |

## ✨ Features

- 🌈 **Drifting aurora background**: soft, animated colour blobs with a film-grain texture
- 🔤 **Letter-by-letter headline animation** with a shimmering gradient
- ⌨️ **Typewriter terminal** cycling through Python, JavaScript, C++, HTML and Bash
- 🖱️ **Cursor-following glow** that tracks your mouse
- 🪟 **Frosted-glass cards and navigation**
- 📜 **Scroll-reveal animations** on the About and Contact pages
- 📱 **Fully responsive**: works on phones, tablets and desktops
- ♿ **Respects "reduced motion"** settings for accessibility

## 🛠️ Built With

- **HTML5**: page structure
- **CSS3**: animations, gradients, glassmorphism, responsive layout
- **JavaScript**: typewriter effect, scroll reveals, form handling
- **Google Fonts**: *Fraunces* and *JetBrains Mono*
- **Netlify**: hosting
